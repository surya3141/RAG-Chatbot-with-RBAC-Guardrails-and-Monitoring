# Q3 2024 Financial Report

## Executive Summary

The company achieved strong financial performance in Q3 2024 with revenue of $15.2 million,
representing a 12% year-over-year growth. Operating expenses were $9.8 million, resulting
in an operating profit of $5.4 million.

## Revenue Breakdown

- Product Sales: $10.1 million (66%)
- Service Revenue: $3.8 million (25%)
- Licensing: $1.3 million (9%)

## Cost Structure

| Category             | Amount (USD) | % of Revenue |
|----------------------|-------------|--------------|
| Cost of Goods Sold   | $4.2M       | 27.6%        |
| R&D                  | $2.1M       | 13.8%        |
| Sales & Marketing    | $1.9M       | 12.5%        |
| General & Admin      | $1.6M       | 10.5%        |

## Cash Flow

- Operating Cash Flow: $6.1 million
- Capital Expenditures: $1.2 million
- Free Cash Flow: $4.9 million

## Quarterly Highlights

1. Launched new enterprise product line adding $1.5M in new ARR.
2. Expanded into three new international markets.
3. Reduced customer churn from 5.2% to 3.8%.

Revenue target: $16.5 million
