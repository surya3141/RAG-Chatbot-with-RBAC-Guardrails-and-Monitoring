# Company Strategy – FY 2025 Plan

## Vision

Become the market-leading enterprise platform for AI-powered workflow automation
with $100M ARR by end of FY 2026.

## Strategic Pillars

### 1. Product Excellence
- Launch v3.0 platform with native AI capabilities (Q1 2025)
- Achieve SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certifications (Q2 2025)
- Launch mobile application for field teams (Q3 2025)
- Expand API ecosystem with 50+ integrations (Q4 2025)

### 2. Market Expansion
- Enter APAC market with Singapore hub as regional HQ
- Expand European operations with new Germany office
- Target Fortune 1000 enterprise segment as primary growth driver
- Launch partner channel programme with 100 certified partners

### 3. Talent & Culture
- Grow headcount from 400 to 600 by end of FY 2025
- Launch leadership development programme for L4+ employees
- Achieve eNPS of +55 or above
- Open offices in Austin (TX) and Amsterdam (NL)

### 4. Operational Excellence
- Achieve 99.99% platform uptime SLA
- Reduce customer onboarding time from 45 days to 14 days
- Implement AI-driven customer support to handle 60% of tickets automatically
- Deploy multi-region infrastructure in US, EU, and APAC

## Financial Targets FY 2025

| Metric               | Target         |
|----------------------|----------------|
| Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) | $68M |
| Revenue Growth       | 55% YoY        |
| Gross Margin         | ≥72%           |
| Operating Margin     | 15%            |
| Net Revenue Retention| ≥120%          |
| Customer Count       | 850+           |

## Key Risks

1. Macroeconomic headwinds reducing enterprise IT budgets
2. Increased competition from hyperscalers entering the market
3. Talent acquisition in a competitive market
4. Regulatory changes affecting AI products (EU AI Act compliance)
