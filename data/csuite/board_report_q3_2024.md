# Board Report – Q3 2024

## Executive Summary

The company delivered another strong quarter with all key metrics trending positively.
Revenue grew 12% YoY to $15.2M while maintaining disciplined cost management.
Headcount expanded by a net 15 employees to 400 total.

## Key Performance Indicators

| KPI                      | Q3 2024  | Q3 2023  | Change  |
|--------------------------|----------|----------|---------|
| Revenue                  | $15.2M   | $13.6M   | +12%    |
| ARR                      | $44M     | $36M     | +22%    |
| Gross Margin             | 71%      | 68%      | +3pp    |
| Operating Income         | $5.4M    | $4.0M    | +35%    |
| Customer Count           | 620      | 510      | +22%    |
| NRR                      | 115%     | 108%     | +7pp    |
| Employee Count           | 400      | 345      | +16%    |
| eNPS                     | +42      | +35      | +7      |

## Strategic Updates

### Product
- Released platform v2.5 with AI workflow suggestions feature
- Beta programme for v3.0 launched with 25 enterprise customers
- 99.97% uptime achieved (SLA: 99.9%)

### Commercial
- Signed 8 new enterprise contracts averaging $185K ACV
- Expanded 12 existing accounts for total expansion revenue of $2.1M
- Lost 3 customers (churn) representing $0.4M ARR

### People
- Promoted 22 employees across levels
- Launched engineering leadership development programme
- Opened recruitment for APAC expansion
