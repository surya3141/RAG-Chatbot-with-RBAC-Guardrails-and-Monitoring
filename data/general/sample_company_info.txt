About TechCorp Inc.
===================

COMPANY OVERVIEW
----------------
TechCorp Inc. is a B2B software company specialising in enterprise data analytics and
AI-powered workflow automation. Founded in 2020 and headquartered in San Francisco, CA,
TechCorp serves over 200 enterprise customers across North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific.

Mission Statement
  "To deliver innovative, secure, and intelligent software solutions that empower
   organisations to make better decisions and work more effectively."

Vision
  To become the leading provider of AI-native enterprise automation by 2028.

Core Values
  1. Customer Obsession — Everything we do starts with our customers' needs.
  2. Integrity — We act with honesty and transparency in all interactions.
  3. Innovation — We embrace change and continuously improve our products.
  4. Inclusion — We build diverse teams and foster a culture of belonging.
  5. Excellence — We hold ourselves to the highest standards of quality.

--------------------
1. TechCorp Insights Platform
   - Real-time business intelligence dashboards
   - Predictive analytics powered by proprietary ML models
   - Integrations with Salesforce, SAP, Workday, and 50+ data sources

2. TechCorp Automate
   - Low-code workflow automation builder
   - AI document processing (OCR + NLP extraction)
   - Robotic Process Automation (RPA) capabilities

3. Professional Services
   - Implementation consulting (average engagement: 6–12 weeks)
   - Data engineering and migration services
   - Custom model development
