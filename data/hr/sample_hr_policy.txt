SECTION 1: LEAVE POLICIES
--------------------------
Annual Leave
  - Full-time employees: 20 days per calendar year
  - Part-time employees: Pro-rated based on contracted hours
  - Leave accrual begins from the first day of employment
  - Maximum carryover: 5 days into the following calendar year
  - Unused leave beyond carryover limit is forfeited

Sick Leave
  - Entitlement: 10 days per calendar year
  - Sick leave does not accumulate; unused days reset annually
  - A medical certificate is required for absences exceeding 3 consecutive days
  - Compassionate leave: Up to 5 days per bereavement event

Parental Leave
  - Primary caregiver: 16 weeks fully paid
  - Secondary caregiver: 4 weeks fully paid
  - Applicable from the first day of employment with no tenure requirement

Public Holidays
  - All federally recognised public holidays are observed
  - Employees required to work on public holidays receive 1.5x overtime pay

SECTION 2: COMPENSATION & PAYROLL
-----------------------------------
Payroll Processing
  - Payroll is processed on the 25th of each calendar month
  - If the 25th falls on a weekend or public holiday, payment is made on the preceding
    business day
  - All employees are paid via direct bank transfer

Salary Review
  - Annual salary review conducted in December of each year
  - Merit increases are effective from January 1 of the following year
  - Market benchmarking is conducted using industry salary surveys

Overtime
  - Non-exempt employees: Overtime paid at 1.5x for hours beyond 40/week
  - Exempt employees: Compensatory time-off awarded at manager discretion

Expense Reimbursement
  - Business expenses must be submitted within 30 days of incurrence
  - Receipts required for all expenses over $25
  - Reimbursements processed with the following month's payroll

SECTION 3: PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT
------------------------------------
Performance Review Cycle
  - Reviews are conducted on a bi-annual basis: June and December
  - Mid-year check-in: June (informal, developmental focus)
  - Year-end review: December (formal, compensation-linked)

Rating Scale
  - 5 — Exceptional: Consistently exceeds all expectations
  - 4 — Exceeds Expectations: Regularly surpasses requirements
  - 3 — Meets Expectations: Consistently fulfils role requirements
  - 2 — Needs Improvement: Partially meets expectations; improvement plan required
  - 1 — Unsatisfactory: Fails to meet expectations; subject to disciplinary action

Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)
  - Employees rated 2 or below are placed on a 90-day PIP
  - PIP outlines specific, measurable improvement objectives
  - Failure to meet PIP objectives may result in termination

SECTION 4: BENEFITS & PERKS
------------------------------
Health Insurance
  - Medical, dental, and vision coverage provided from day 1
  - TechCorp covers 80% of employee premiums; 60% for dependants

Retirement
  - 401(k) plan with 4% employer match (vesting: 2-year cliff)
  - Plan administered by Fidelity Investments

Additional Perks
  - Remote work: Up to 3 days per week (role-dependent)
  - Home office stipend: $500 one-time upon joining; $200 annual refresh
  - Learning & development budget: $1,500 per employee per year
  - Gym/wellness reimbursement: Up to $50/month
  - Team lunch budget: $25/person/month

SECTION 5: CODE OF CONDUCT
-----------------------------
All employees are expected to:
  - Act with integrity and professionalism at all times
  - Maintain confidentiality of company and client information
  - Report conflicts of interest to HR and management
  - Treat all colleagues with respect; zero tolerance for harassment
  - Comply with all applicable laws and company policies

SECTION 6: TRAINING & DEVELOPMENT
------------------------------------
Mandatory Training
  - Information Security Awareness: Annually
  - Anti-Harassment & Workplace Safety: Annually
  - Data Privacy (GDPR/CCPA): Annually for all staff handling personal data

Development Programmes
  - Manager training: New managers complete a 6-week leadership programme
  - Mentoring: Formal mentoring programme pairs juniors with senior staff
  - Conference attendance: Supported for high performers (budget permitting)

SECTION 7: RECRUITMENT & ONBOARDING
--------------------------------------
Hiring Process
  - All vacancies posted internally for a minimum of 5 business days
  - External job boards: LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor
  - Interview panels must include at least one member from HR

Onboarding
  - 30-60-90 day plan provided to all new hires on Day 1
  - Buddy system: Each new hire assigned a peer buddy for the first 90 days
  - IT equipment provisioned before the start date

SECTION 8: DISCIPLINARY PROCEDURES
-------------------------------------
Progressive Discipline
  1. Verbal warning (documented in employee file)
  2. Written warning
  3. Final written warning
  4. Termination

Gross Misconduct
  - Acts of gross misconduct (e.g., fraud, harassment, data breach) may result in
    immediate termination without prior warnings
