# HR Policy Manual – 2024 Edition

## 1. Employment Policies

### 1.1 Equal Opportunity Employment
The company is an equal opportunity employer. All employment decisions are based
on qualifications, merit, and business needs without regard to race, color,
religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, or any other protected class.

### 1.2 Working Hours
Standard working hours are 9 AM to 5 PM, Monday through Friday.
Flexible work arrangements are available with manager approval.
Remote work is permitted up to 3 days per week for eligible roles.

### 1.3 Probation Period
All new employees serve a 90-day probation period during which performance
is closely monitored and formal feedback is provided at 30, 60, and 90 days.

## 2. Leave Policies

### 2.1 Paid Time Off (PTO)
- Years 0–2: 15 days per year
- Years 3–5: 18 days per year
- Years 6+: 22 days per year

### 2.2 Sick Leave
All employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year.
Unused sick leave does not carry over.

### 2.3 Parental Leave
Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave.
Secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks of paid parental leave.

## 3. Performance Management

### 3.1 Review Cycle
Performance reviews are conducted semi-annually (June and December).
Mid-year check-ins are mandatory for all employees.

### 3.2 Rating Scale
- Exceeds Expectations
- Meets Expectations
- Needs Improvement
- Unsatisfactory

## 4. Code of Conduct

All employees must adhere to the company Code of Conduct, which prohibits:
- Harassment or discrimination of any kind
- Misuse of company resources
- Conflicts of interest without disclosure
- Disclosure of confidential information
