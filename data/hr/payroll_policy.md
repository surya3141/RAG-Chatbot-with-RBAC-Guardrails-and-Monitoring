# Payroll Policy & Compensation Guide

## 1. Payroll Schedule

Employees are paid bi-weekly (every other Friday).
Pay periods run Sunday through Saturday.
Direct deposit is mandatory for all employees.

## 2. Compensation Bands

| Level  | Role               | Annual Salary Range       |
|--------|--------------------|---------------------------|
| L1     | Associate          | $55,000 – $70,000         |
| L2     | Mid-level          | $70,000 – $90,000         |
| L3     | Senior             | $90,000 – $120,000        |
| L4     | Staff / Lead       | $120,000 – $160,000       |
| L5     | Principal / Manager| $160,000 – $210,000       |
| L6     | Director           | $210,000 – $270,000       |
| L7     | VP                 | $270,000 – $380,000       |

## 3. Bonus Structure

- Annual performance bonus: 5–20% of base salary based on performance rating.
- Company-wide bonus pool: distributed when company hits >110% of revenue target.
- Spot bonuses: up to $5,000 with VP approval.

## 4. Benefits Summary

| Benefit                | Details                                        |
|------------------------|------------------------------------------------|
| Health Insurance       | Medical, dental, vision – 90% employer paid   |
| 401(k)                 | 4% employer match, vested after 2 years        |
| Stock Options          | Granted at hire, 4-year vest with 1-year cliff |
| Life Insurance         | 2× annual salary                               |
| Professional Development| $2,000 per year                              |
| Home Office Stipend    | $500 one-time + $50/month for remote employees |

## 5. Payroll Deductions

Standard deductions include:
- Federal and state income tax (withholding per W-4)
- FICA (Social Security and Medicare)
- Health insurance premiums
- 401(k) contributions (pre-tax)

## 6. Overtime Policy

Non-exempt employees are eligible for overtime pay at 1.5× their hourly rate
for hours worked beyond 40 per week.
